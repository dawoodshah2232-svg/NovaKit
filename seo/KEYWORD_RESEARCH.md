# Keyword Research — pdfedit.website (2026-09-23)

Method: public web research (search-result PAA harvesting, competitor blog scans of
ilovepdf.com/blog and smallpdf.com/blog, trend writeups). Google Trends direct fetch
was JS-blocked; seasonality below reflects well-known recurring patterns, not measured
Trends data. Demand tiers are RELATIVE estimates triangulated from SERP competition,
autocomplete/PAA density and published keyword studies — not exact monthly volumes.
No exact volume numbers are claimed anywhere in this file.

## Seasonality notes (typical patterns)
- Evergreen core: merge / compress / pdf↔word / sign — steady all year.
- Jan–Apr (tax season): merge, split, sign, redact spike (tax docs, contracts).
- Aug–Sep (back-to-school / hiring): CV/resume, compress, jpg→pdf, OCR spike.
- Year-end: invoice generator, sign (contracts), e-sign legality questions spike.

## What makes content citable by AI assistants

AI answers (ChatGPT, Perplexity, AI Overviews, Bing Copilot) cite pages that behave like
answer databases: open with a direct 40–60-word answer to the exact question, use the
question verbatim as an H2, follow with numbered steps and short paragraphs, and add
comparison tables where choices exist (e.g. password vs redact vs flatten). Mark it up with
FAQPage + HowTo schema, keep a visible "Last reviewed" date, link the live tool inline at
the step where it's used, and state honest limits (what the free browser tool can't do).
One question per H2, no keyword stuffing, no fake claims — assistants penalize thin or
dishonest pages by simply not citing them.
